# Hotel Booking & Revenue Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 1. Project Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze hotel booking performance, revenue, cancellations, room occupancy, and pricing trends. The project helps hotel management monitor business performance and make data-driven decisions.

## 2. Business Objectives
- Analyze total bookings and revenue.
- Monitor cancellation trends and rates.
- Calculate room occupancy and Average Daily Rate (ADR).
- Compare City Hotel and Resort Hotel performance.
- Identify monthly booking and revenue trends.
- Analyze customer behavior and booking channels.

## 3. Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel / CSV
- Data Modeling and Visualization

## 4. Implementation
- **Data Cleaning:** Removed duplicates, handled missing values, and corrected data types using Power Query.
- **Data Transformation:** Prepared and structured data for analysis.
- **Data Modeling:** Created relationships and a calendar table.
- **DAX Calculations:** Developed measures for bookings, revenue, cancellations, ADR, and occupancy.

## 5. Key KPIs
- Total Bookings
- Total Revenue
- Total Cancellations
- Cancellation Rate
- Average Daily Rate (ADR)
- Average Length of Stay
- Room Occupancy Rate

## 6. Dashboard Features
- Booking and revenue analysis.
- Hotel-wise performance comparison.
- Monthly booking and revenue trends.
- Cancellation analysis.
- Customer and market segment analysis.
- Interactive slicers, KPI cards, drill-downs, and tooltips.

## 7. Business Insights
The dashboard helps identify revenue trends, high-demand periods, cancellation patterns, profitable customer segments, and hotel occupancy performance. These insights support better pricing strategies, booking management, and revenue optimization.

## 8. Conclusion
Successfully transformed raw hotel booking data into an interactive Power BI dashboard, demonstrating practical skills in data cleaning, Power Query, data modeling, DAX, KPI development, and business analysis.
